# Artist positioning on the first screen

## What We’re Building

Both desktop and mobile hero sections should identify Ekaterina Sokolova as an artist before presenting the themes of her work. The artist’s name remains the single semantic H1 and the largest visual element.

## Approaches Considered

1. Direct professional positioning: “Современная художница · Москва” / “Екатерина Соколова” / “Авторская живопись · Урбанизм · Цветы”.
2. Gallery-first positioning: “Художница Екатерина Соколова” / “Онлайн-галерея авторской живописи”.
3. Poetic positioning: “Художница города и цвета” / “Екатерина Соколова” / “Москва · Архитектура · Цветы”.

## Approved Direction

Use a direct factual identifier together with language derived from the artist’s own statement. Keep generic descriptions of the website out of the visible hero so the artist’s identity remains primary.

## Key Decisions

- H1: “Екатерина Соколова”.
- Eyebrow: “Современный российский художник”.
- Place the eyebrow close to the artist's name so it reads as one identity lockup, not as a detached navigation label.
- Preserve the original Bodoni/Didot display character and its stronger contrast across hero and section headings; keep safer line-height and reveal the full glyph outlines after the entrance animation.
- Descriptor: “Живопись между реализмом и мистикой”.
- Keep “Онлайн-галерея авторской живописи” in search metadata, not as visible hero copy.
- Remove the redundant visible label “Художественная галерея”.
- Do not use a second H1 or a competing H2 in the hero.
- Keep the artwork, gallery CTA and current asymmetric composition.
