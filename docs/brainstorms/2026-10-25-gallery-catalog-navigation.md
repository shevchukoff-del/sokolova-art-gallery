# Gallery and catalog terminology

## What We’re Building

Use separate, consistent names for the two ways of viewing the artist’s work: “Галерея” for the immersive 3D exhibition and “Каталог работ” for the filterable collection and archive.

## Why This Approach

“Галерея работ” makes the catalog page sound like a second version of the 3D gallery. “Каталог работ” describes its actual structure—cards, availability states, archive and filters—and makes the navigation choice clear.

## Key Decisions

- Keep “Галерея” for the interactive 3D route.
- Use “Каталог работ” in navigation, page headings and mobile collection labels.
- On desktop, navigation underlines appear on hover only.
- Keep `aria-current="page"` for assistive technology without a persistent visual underline.
